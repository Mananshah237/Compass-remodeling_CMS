## Logging In

1. Navigate to `/admin/login` on your website
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. You'll be redirected to the admin dashboard

**Note:** Only users with admin access can log in. If you need admin access, contact your website administrator.

## Managing Services

Services are the main offerings you display on your website (e.g., "Kitchen Remodeling", "Bathroom Renovation").

### Adding a New Service

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Services"**
2. Click the **"Add Service"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Service Title** (required): e.g., "Kitchen Remodeling"
   - **Description** (required): Describe what this service includes
   - **Image**: Click "Choose File" to upload an image (recommended: 800x600px or larger)
4. Click **"Create Service"**
5. Your service will appear on the public website immediately!

### Editing a Service

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Services"**
2. Find the service you want to edit
3. Click the **"Edit"** button
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Update Service"**
6. Changes appear on the website immediately!

### Deleting a Service

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Services"**
2. Find the service you want to delete
3. Click the **"Delete"** button
4. Confirm the deletion in the pop-up dialog
5. The service is removed from the website immediately

## Managing Gallery

The gallery displays images of your completed projects.

### Adding a Gallery Image

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Gallery"**
2. Click the **"Add Gallery Item"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Image** (required): Click "Choose File" to upload an image (recommended: 1200x800px or larger)
   - **Caption** (optional): Add a description or project name
4. Click **"Add Gallery Item"**
5. The image appears on the gallery page immediately!

### Editing a Gallery Item

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Gallery"**
2. Find the gallery item you want to edit
3. Click the **"Edit"** button
4. Update the image or caption
5. Click **"Update Gallery Item"**

### Deleting a Gallery Item

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Gallery"**
2. Find the gallery item you want to delete
3. Click the **"Delete"** button
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The image is removed from the gallery immediately

## Managing Testimonials

Testimonials are video reviews from your clients (YouTube or Vimeo).

### Adding a Testimonial

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Testimonials"**
2. Click the **"Add Testimonial"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Client Name** (optional): The client's name
   - **Video URL** (required): Paste the YouTube or Vimeo video URL
     - Example: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`
     - Example: `https://vimeo.com/...`
4. Click **"Add Testimonial"**
5. The testimonial appears on your website immediately!

### Editing a Testimonial

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Testimonials"**
2. Find the testimonial you want to edit
3. Click the **"Edit"** button
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Update Testimonial"**

### Deleting a Testimonial

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"Manage Testimonials"**
2. Find the testimonial you want to delete
3. Click the **"Delete"** button
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The testimonial is removed from the website immediately

## Viewing Messages

Messages are submissions from your contact form.

### Viewing Messages

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"View Messages"**
2. You'll see all messages submitted through your contact form
3. Each message shows:
   - Name and email
   - Phone number (if provided)
   - Message content
   - Date and time received

### Deleting a Message

1. Go to **Admin Dashboard** → Click **"View Messages"**
2. Find the message you want to delete
3. Click the **"Delete"** button
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The message is permanently removed

**Note:** Deleting a message is permanent. Make sure you've saved any important information before deleting.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Images

- **Recommended sizes:**
  - Service images: 800x600px or larger
  - Gallery images: 1200x800px or larger
- **File formats:** JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **File size:** Keep images under 5MB for faster loading
- **Quality:** Use high-quality images that showcase your work

### Content

- **Services:** Write clear, concise descriptions. Focus on what makes your service valuable.
- **Gallery:** Add captions to help visitors understand the project.
- **Testimonials:** Use authentic video testimonials from satisfied clients.

### Publishing

- **No redeploy needed:** All changes appear on your website immediately after saving!
- **Preview:** You can always visit your public website to see how changes look.
- **Backup:** Important content is stored safely in the database.

### Security

- **Never share your login credentials**
- **Log out when finished:** Click "Logout" in the admin area when done
- **Use strong passwords:** Keep your admin account secure

## Troubleshooting

### Can't log in?

- Make sure you're using the correct email and password
- Contact your administrator if you need to reset your password
- Ensure you have admin access

### Images not uploading?

- Check that the file size is under 5MB
- Make sure the file is in JPG, PNG, or WebP format
- Try a different browser if the issue persists

### Changes not appearing?

- Refresh your browser page
- Clear your browser cache
- Make sure you clicked "Save" or "Update"

### Need help?

Contact your website administrator or developer for assistance.
